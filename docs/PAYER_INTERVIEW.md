# Real payer conversation guide - Week 14

## Before showing Primer Peldaño

Ask the budget owner to reconstruct one recent opening:

1. How many applications reached the first screen?
2. Who reviewed them, and how much loaded time did each review require?
3. How many clarification cycles occurred and who handled them?
4. Which external tools, agencies, tests, or job boards were paid for?
5. What evidence caused a proceed, clarify, or decline decision?
6. Which applicants may have been excluded because their experience came from unfamiliar or informal settings?
7. What cost of vacancy can the payer defend from its own records, and what is only an assumption?
8. Who owns the budget and what alternative would they buy instead?

Do not reveal MX$12,000 until the current process and alternative are understood.

## Offer to present

> For one real opening, Primer Peldaño would publish frozen criteria and accepted evidence, allocate five review slots after essential eligibility checks, accept only worker-authorized submissions, support five criterion-linked human decisions, and commit to a declared response deadline. Workers pay MX$0. The employer price to test is MX$12,000.

Then ask:

> Are you willing to identify one opening, introduce the budget owner, and schedule a decision meeting for this exact offer?

## Evidence ladder

Record exactly one outcome:

- Rejection with reason
- Interest without an opening or owner
- Named opening and decision-maker
- Written proposal requested
- Pilot refused after proposal
- Signed pilot
- Paid pilot

A compliment, hypothetical yes, or request to “stay in touch” is not demand.
